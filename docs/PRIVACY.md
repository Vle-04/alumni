# How we use your information

This notice explains, in plain language, what the Academy's Alumni Tracker keeps about you, why we keep it, who can see it, and how long we keep it. If anything is unclear, contact the Academy careers team.

## Why this exists

We want to know whether our graduates find jobs, so we can improve our programs and help graduates who are still searching. We also share job openings from partner companies with graduates who might be a good fit.

## What we collect

- **Who you are:** your name, and the program and cohort you studied in, with your graduation date.
- **How to reach you:** your email address, and optionally your phone number. You can also add a LinkedIn link; we store it as text only.
- **Your career updates:**
  - whether you're working, studying or looking for work
  - the company and job title
  - when you started
  - whether the job is in your field
- **Salary, only if you choose to share it:** as a range ("band"), never an exact amount.
- **Job-search preferences:** the kinds of roles you want, where you can work (remote, hybrid, on-site, relocation) and your skills, rated by you.
- **Your choices:** which uses of your data you have agreed to (see below), and whether you want check-in emails.
- **Survey history:** when we sent you a check-in, and whether you answered.

**What we never collect:** your age, date of birth, gender, nationality, ethnicity, religion, photo or any similar personal characteristic. They are never used to match you with jobs.

**We never look you up elsewhere.** We never copy information from LinkedIn or any other website. Everything comes from you, or from our careers team when you tell them something directly.

## Your choices (consent)

You can switch each of these on or off at any time on your personal page. A change takes effect immediately.

| Choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| Include my anonymous outcomes in Academy statistics | Your updates count towards figures such as "71% of graduates were employed after a year". You're never named in them. |
| Match me with job openings | Our careers team can suggest openings that fit your skills and preferences. |
| Share my profile with a partner company when I say I'm interested | If you click **I'm interested** on an opening, we may share your name, email, skills and LinkedIn link with that company. Nothing is shared unless you've clicked Interested **and** this choice is on. |
| Contact me about featuring my story | We may ask, separately, whether you'd like to share your story. |

## Who can see your information

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **You** | Everything we hold about you, through your personal link. You can download it at any time. |
| **The Academy careers team** | Your profile and history, so they can help you. Your email and phone are hidden until they choose to view them, and every view is recorded. |
| **Academy leadership and marketing** | Only totals and percentages, never your name, email or salary. Any group smaller than 5 people is hidden so nobody can be singled out. |
| **A partner company** | Only if you clicked **I'm interested** on their opening **and** turned on sharing with partners. Then they see your name, email, skills and LinkedIn link, and nothing else. If you turn sharing off, they stop seeing it straight away. |

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

## How we keep it safe

- Your email, phone number and LinkedIn link are encrypted in our database.
- Your personal link expires after 30 days. If it falls into the wrong hands, the careers team can cancel it.
- Changing your email, downloading your data or deleting your account needs a code we send to your email.
- We record who looked at your contact details and every report export, so access can be checked.
- Our application logs never contain your personal details.

## How long we keep it

- **While you're a graduate:** we keep your record so we can follow how your career develops. Check-ins happen at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months after graduation; after that, you update only if you want to.
- **5 years after graduation:** your record is anonymised automatically. We remove your name and contact details and keep only anonymous facts (for example "a graduate of this cohort started a job in March"), so our historical statistics stay correct.
- **Access logs** are kept for 3 years. **Email delivery logs** are kept for 90 days.

## When you delete your account

- **Records about you are edited.** We keep a log of who did what, so access to your data can be checked. That log is normally never changed. Deleting your account is the one exception: we go back through the log and remove your name, email address, phone number and the internet (IP) addresses of your own visits. We keep only the fact that something happened ("a graduate's record was viewed"). Otherwise the log would be a copy of the very data you asked us to delete.
- **Text that mentions you is cleaned.** Where your name, email or phone appear in text you or staff typed (for example a job title, a company name you entered, or a job description written for you), they are replaced with "[removed]".
- **Copies outside our database.**
  - **Email provider:** the service that delivers our emails keeps its own delivery logs (recipient address, subject, delivery time) for **[ACADEMY TO FILL IN: e.g. 30 days, per our email provider's policy]**. We cannot delete those early.
  - **Database backups:** our backups contain your data until they expire, after **[ACADEMY TO FILL IN: e.g. 35 days]**. Backups are encrypted and only restored to recover from a failure. If one is ever restored, we delete your data again before it is used.
- **Shared internet connections.** To stop people guessing links and codes, we briefly block an internet address after too many wrong attempts. On a shared connection (a campus or mobile network), one person's mistakes can briefly block others too. Wait a few minutes or ask for a new link.

## Your rights

On your personal page you can:

- **See and correct** your information.
- **Download** everything we hold about you (as a JSON file).
- **Change your choices** at any time.
- **Stop check-in emails**: there's also a link at the bottom of every check-in email.
- **Delete your account.** We remove your name, contact details, preferences and skills straight away. We keep only anonymous facts that can't be linked back to you, so our statistics don't change. This can't be undone.

If you've lost your link, use **"Send me a new link"** on the expired-link page. You can also ask the careers team to do any of the above for you.
